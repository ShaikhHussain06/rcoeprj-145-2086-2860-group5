1 Project Objective & Clinical Problem Statement

Primary Goal: Provide an automated, high-precision acoustic screening platform for acoustic stethoscope recordings to reliably detect pathological heart murmurs, valvular regurgitation (e.g., Mitral Regurgitation), and stenotic ejection turbulence (e.g., Aortic Stenosis) prior to secondary echocardiogram referral.

  • Clinical Challenge: Traditional cardiac stethoscope auscultation is highly subjective. Subtle or early-stage murmurs are frequently missed in high-noise clinic environments, leading to delayed cardiac interventions.
  • System Solution: CardioSense implements a deterministic pipeline: raw acoustic ingestion → zero-phase 4th-order Butterworth DSP filtering → 128-band Log-Mel Spectrogram extraction → PyTorch CardioResNet-18 deep inference → Youden-calibrated optimal thresholding → safety-guardrailed clinical report generation.

3 Audio Ingestion & Resampling Protocol

  • Internal DSP Sample Rate: Standardized to 2,000 Hz ($f_s = 2,000\text{ Hz}$).
    • Nyquist Limit: $f_{Nyquist} = 1,000\text{ Hz}$ ($f_s / 2$). Fundamental heart valve thuds ($S_1, S_2$) span 20–150 Hz; pathological murmurs span up to 400 Hz. A 2,000 Hz rate captures 100% of the relevant acoustic envelope without wasting computational memory on unnecessary high frequencies.
  • Channel Downmix: Multi-channel recordings are downmixed to single-channel mono by averaging: $y_{mono}[n] = \frac{1}{C}\sum_{c=1}^C y_c[n]$.
  • Universal Browser Playback Resampling: Raw PhysioNet recordings are sampled at 2,000 Hz float64, which native browser decoders (Chrome/Edge) reject with NotSupportedError. The backend server automatically resamples audio on-the-fly to 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV via scipy.signal.resample_poly (39ms execution), enabling instant, crackle-free browser playback.

4 Digital Signal Processing (DSP) & Bandpass Filtering

Filter Implementation: 4th-Order Butterworth Bandpass IIR Filter via scipy.signal.butter & scipy.signal.filtfilt.

  • Lowcut Frequency ($f_{low}$): 20.0 Hz — Strips sensor friction against skin, thoracic breathing movement, muscle tremors, and baseline drift.
  • Highcut Frequency ($f_{high}$): 200.0 Hz — Strips environmental room chatter, clinician voices, equipment noise, and 50/60 Hz mains electrical hum.
  • Zero-Phase Filtering (filtfilt): Applies the filter forward and reverse. Result: Zero phase distortion ($\Delta \phi = 0$). Crucial for clinical medicine because causal filters delay high frequencies, distorting the exact time alignment of $S_1$ (mitral/tricuspid) and $S_2$ (aortic/pulmonic) valve closures.
  • Peak Amplitude Normalization: Scales signal amplitude to $[-1.0, 1.0]$: $y_{norm}[n] = \frac{y[n]}{\max(|y|) + 10^{-6}}$.

5 Temporal Segmentation: Why 4.0-Second Windows with 50% Overlap?

CardioSense splits filtered recordings into 4.0-second sliding windows (8,000 samples) with a 2.0-second hop (50% overlap):

  • Physiological Verification (4–6 Complete Cardiac Cycles): At a resting adult heart rate of 60–100 BPM, one cardiac cycle ($S_1 \rightarrow \text{systole} \rightarrow S_2 \rightarrow \text{diastole}$) lasts 0.6–1.0s. A 4.0-second window guarantees 4 to 6 full beats. Shorter windows (e.g. 1.0s) risk catching only half a beat, making rhythm and continuous turbulence indistinguishable.
  • Temporal Murmur Localization: Slicing allows the system to pinpoint exactly when pathology occurs (e.g., "Segment 3 (4.0s–8.0s) flagged: elevated turbulence"), rather than providing only a blind overall label.
  • Prevents Murmur Dilution: If a patient has a transient 4-second murmur in a 35-second recording, evaluating the whole recording at once dilutes the murmur energy. Slicing isolates the anomaly with high signal-to-noise ratio.
  • 50% Boundary Overlap (2.0s Hop): Prevents murmurs that occur right at a boundary cut (e.g. at second 3.9) from being bisected and missed. Every beat appears fully centered in at least one window.
  • Uniform Matrix Dimensions: Produces uniform 2D tensors of shape (1, 128, 63) for fast batch parallel inference.

7 Deep Learning Architecture: CardioResNet-18

The primary inference backbone is CardioResNet-18, an 18-layer deep residual convolutional neural network:

  • Stem: $7\times 7$ Conv2D (64 filters, stride 2) → BatchNorm → ReLU → $3\times 3$ MaxPool (stride 2).
  • 4 Residual Stages (8 Residual Blocks):
    • Stage 1: 64 channels (stride 1) • Stage 2: 128 channels (stride 2 downsampling)
    • Stage 3: 256 channels (stride 2 downsampling) • Stage 4: 512 channels (stride 2 downsampling)
  • Residual Basic Block: $\text{Output} = \text{ReLU}\Big(\text{Conv}_2(\text{ReLU}(\text{Conv}_1(x))) + \text{Shortcut}(x)\Big)$. Identity skip connections allow gradients to flow directly without vanishing, preserving delicate acoustic murmur signatures.
  • Global Pooling & Regularization: AdaptiveAvgPool2d((1, 1)) collapses the spatial dimensions, enabling the model to handle recordings of variable length without resizing artifacts. Regularized with Dropout ($p = 0.35$) and Batch Normalization.
  • Classifier: Fully-connected layer mapping 512 features to 2 class logits (Normal vs. Abnormal).

8 Optimal Decision Threshold ($\tau^* = 0.4900$) & Score Ranges

The network outputs an Abnormal Probability Score ($P$) between $0.00$ and $1.00$. In clinical medicine, a false negative (missing a diseased heart) is catastrophic. Using Youden's J Statistic ($J = \text{Sensitivity} + \text{Specificity} - 1$), the decision threshold was calibrated to $\tau^* = 0.4900$ (49.0%):

9 Clinical AI Report Agent & Medical Safety Guardrails

Every inference result is processed through an automated safety and governance layer before presentation:

  • Anti-Overclaiming Filter: Regular expressions replace definitive diagnostic claims:
    • "You have aortic stenosis" → "Acoustic patterns indicate potential signs of aortic stenosis".
    • "Confirmed case of heart failure" → "Presents acoustic characteristics consistent with...".
  • Hallucination / Grounding Check:
    • If $P(\text{Abnormal}) < 0.35$, words like "critical murmur" or "severe pathology" are strictly suppressed.
    • If $P(\text{Abnormal}) > 0.65$, terms like "completely healthy heart" or "zero abnormalities" are prohibited.
  • Mandatory Investigational Disclaimer: Every generated summary appends a statutory reminder: "CardioSense is an automated acoustic screening and decision-support tool. It does not provide a definitive diagnosis. All findings must be clinically correlated with stethoscope auscultation and echocardiography by a licensed clinician."

10 Cryptographic Security & Audit Logging

To guarantee medical audit compliance, reproducibility, and chain-of-custody:

  • SHA-256 Audio Signature: Upon upload, a 256-bit cryptographic hash of raw audio bytes is generated.
  • SQLite Persistence (cardiosense.db):
    • AnalysisRecord: Stores session ID (CS-XXXXXXXXXXXX), patient tag, confidence, duration, and findings.
    • AuditLog: Records model version, filter frequencies, raw segment probability arrays, and guardrail audit flags.
